Available Tools
🦶
Fall Risk — Posttest Probability Calculator
Calculates cumulative posttest probability of future falls for community-dwelling older adults using Bayesian likelihood ratio chaining. Based on Lusardi et al. (2017) and updated per the 2025 APTA Geriatrics CPG.
Geriatrics Fall Risk Clinical Screening Lusardi 2017 APTA CPG 2025
→
💊
2023 AGS Beers Criteria — PIM Lookup & Drug Interaction Checker
Look up potentially inappropriate medications (PIMs) for older adults per the 2023 American Geriatrics Society Beers Criteria, with drug–drug interaction checking.
Pharmacology Beers Criteria Older Adults AGS 2023
→
🚶
Functional Mobility Test Station
Administer and score mobility, gait, strength, endurance, balance, posture and flexibility tests, plus balance confidence and fear of falling questionnaires. Includes built-in timers, age norms, and a copy-ready chart note. Works on a phone.
Geriatrics Mobility Gait Balance Outcome Measures
→
❤️
HR Max & Target Heart Rate Calculator
Estimate maximal heart rate with the Tanaka and Brawner equations, then set Karvonen (heart rate reserve) training target zones with matching RPE ranges, from an estimated or measured HR max. Includes a beta-blocker option.
Exercise Prescription Heart Rate Karvonen Tanaka Brawner ACSM
